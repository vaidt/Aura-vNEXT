# BOUNDARY INCIDENTS

- **Document status:** FACTUAL RECORD

A log of detected violations of `governance/REPOSITORY-BOUNDARY.md`, and of any discovery
that a frozen source repository was not in fact frozen.

An incident is recorded whether or not it was corrected, and whether or not it was
harmless. **Incidents are never deleted, and never rewritten to look better.**

## Format

```
## INC-NNNN — <one-line summary>
- Date detected:
- Detected by:
- Boundary rule violated:      (REPOSITORY-BOUNDARY.md section)
- What happened:
- Affected register entries:   (set to BLOCKED, or none)
- Corrective action:
- Status: OPEN | CORRECTED
```
